Trade Policy Activity Index

Trade Policy Activity Index

A novel composite indicator measuring the evolution of global trade policy since the Global Financial Crisis, covering 197 countries and territories.

✓  Updated through October 2025 Samuele Centorrino  ·  Antonia Diakantoni  ·  Alexander Keck  ·  Michele Ruta  ·  Monika Sztajerowska  ·  Yuting Wei

About the TPA Index

The Trade Policy Activity (TPA) Index is a composite indicator of global trade policy activity, constructed using a Dynamic Factor Model applied to comprehensive data on trade policy interventions across 197 countries and territories since January 2010. The index documents a structural shift around 2019, reflecting a substantial and persistent expansion in the use of trade policy measures globally.

How has it been constructed?

The TPA Index is estimated using a Dynamic Factor Model (DFM) on a large cross-section of monthly trade policy intervention data sourced from the Global Trade Alert (GTA) and WTO databases. The model extracts a common factor from the co-movement across country- and measure-level series, interpreted as aggregate global trade policy activity. All series are smoothed using a three-month moving average and normalized so that the average equals zero over January 2010–December 2011. The index additionally incorporates Mixed Data Sampling (MIDAS) regressions on high-frequency GTA data to enable real-time nowcasting of trade policy shifts.

What does it include?

The dataset provides eight monthly series from January 2010 to October 2025: a Global Factor (baseline worldwide index); a Facilitating sub-index (trade-liberalizing measures and rollbacks); an Other sub-index (non-facilitating measures including tariff increases, bans, quotas, and subsidies); a Restrictive sub-index (trade-restricting subset of Other); a Remaining other sub-index (largely subsidies and industrial policy); G20 and Non-G20 economy indices; and a GDP-weighted index using pre-sample GDP shares (2005–2009) as time-invariant weights.

Why is it different from other measures?

Existing trade policy measures tend to focus on tariffs alone or rely on annual survey data. The TPA Index covers the full range of policy instruments — tariffs, non-tariff barriers, export restrictions, and industrial policy — at monthly frequency across a near-global sample. Its DFM framework extracts the underlying co-movement rather than simply counting interventions, and the MIDAS nowcasting component enables real-time monitoring well ahead of official data releases.

What are the key findings?

The TPA Index identifies a structural break around 2019 — a persistent expansion in global trade policy activity departing from the post-GFC baseline. Beyond this trend, the index captures cyclical spikes during the 2018–19 US–China trade war, COVID-19 supply chain disruptions, and the post-2022 wave of industrial policy. G20 and non-G20 economies differ significantly in the timing, intensity, and instrument mix of their trade policy responses.
